# Design Slides

## Purpose
Turn the approved lesson structure into clear slide-ready content.

## Procedure
- Build a slide outline from the lesson sections.
- Keep each slide focused on one teaching purpose.
- Add concise speaker notes and visual guidance.
- Preserve source references for factual slide content.

## Required Inputs
- Lesson structure
- Examples and questions, when available
- Source references
- Target education level

## Required Outputs
- Slide outline
- Slide-ready content
- Speaker notes
- Visual guidance
- Slide-to-source coverage map

## Allowed Tools
- `source_citation_lookup`
- `structured_output_validator`
- `workflow_event_writer`

## Quality Gates
- Avoid overloaded slides.
- Keep sequence aligned to the lesson plan.
- Mark derived visuals or analogies clearly.
